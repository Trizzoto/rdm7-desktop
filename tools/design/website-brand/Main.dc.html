RDM website · design style

Foundations

The Studio lap-timer look, lifted value for value from the app. Light ground with a black bar, Barlow Condensed headings in caps, red for where you are and the one thing to do, nowhere else. No rounded corners anywhere.

Corners
None. Zero radius on everything. Only things that are round because they are round things: dots, lamps.
Red
Means where you are (the tab underline, the active mode) and the one action (the primary button). The only data red is a slower delta. Everything else marks itself with ink.
Rules, not shadows
Structure is 1px dividers and ruled tables, one fact per column. No drop shadows on the page. Panel gutters are 10px.
Pictures
A product sits in a panel with a header row, on the dark stage. The product picture is the app itself, drawn as itself.
